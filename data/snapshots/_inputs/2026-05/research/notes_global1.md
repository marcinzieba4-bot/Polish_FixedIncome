# Global PIT facts 30 Apr - 13 May 2026: working notes
Windows (Warsaw publication time, CEST throughout): R0504 = 30 Apr-3 May (1 May holiday, weekend); R0505 = 4 May; R0506 = 5 May; R0507 = 6 May; R0508 = 7 May; R0511 = 8-10 May; R0512 = 11 May; R0513 = 12 May; R0514 = 13 May.
Output: facts.json (144 facts + 13 fed_pricing rows), built by build/make_json.py from build/f_MMDD.py (one file per R, facts F and Fed-pricing rows FP), build/extra.py (pricing objects, CB decisions, CPI, yield tables) and build/digest.py. Schema as in April facts_global_2.json:
- facts: published / window_report_date / topic / text / src; fed_pricing rows: published / text / src / window_report_date.
- fed_pricing_by_R, ecb_pricing, boe_pricing: dicts keyed by R, short text naming the source date.
- cb_decisions: {bank, date, decision, rate, vote, published, note, src}; includes the last pre-window Fed, CNB, MNB, NBR and BoJ decisions so stances are known. Filter on published <= the info cut-off.
- cpi: {country, period, value, core, published, note, src}; EA, Germany, France, US (CPI and PCE), UK, CZ, HU, RO, with the previous prints carried.
- digest (<= 450 chars) and glob (<= 60 chars, leading space), keyed by R.
- yield_tables (extra, as in the April global1 file): the 11 PAP/Bloomberg morning tables 30 Apr-14 May keyed by table time, with a `use` note (fact in window X / levels only for R).
The build checks that every cited PAP, Odaily and TE item was downloaded, that the cited item's first-publication time falls in the fact's window (no mismatches remain), and the length limits. Topics as before; "NBR" = Romania, CZ data under "CNB", HU data under "MNB", Romanian politics/HU politics and yield tables under "sovereign", tariffs under "geopolitics".

## Big picture (as sourced, no hindsight)
- 30 Apr: Brent's expiring June contract spiked above $126 on an Axios report that Trump would be briefed on new strike options and partial control of Hormuz, then fell back (July front month ~$110-114). The ECB held unanimously (DFR 2.00%) but discussed a hike (Lagarde: "certainly moving away from the baseline"); the BoE held 8-1 (Pill for +25bp; Bailey: "active hold", "forceful" response if needed). Euro-area April HICP 3.0% (core 2.2%), Q1 GDP +0.1%; US Q1 GDP 2.0%, March core PCE 3.2%, claims 189k. Japan intervened at ~160.7/USD (yen to <156). Pricing after the meetings: ~75bp of ECB hikes (June fully priced), ~70bp of BoE hikes, no 2026 Fed cut.
- 1-3 May: Iran sent an updated proposal via Pakistan; Trump "not satisfied", told Congress hostilities had "terminated" (War Powers), and announced 25% tariffs on EU cars; Nagel: June ECB hike unless the outlook improves; ISM manufacturing 52.7 with prices at a 2022 high; Hammack/Kashkari: FOMC statement too dovish; OPEC+ +188k b/d for June.
- 4 May: "Project Freedom" (US-guided exit of stranded ships) led to US-Iran exchanges of fire, Iranian missiles/drones on the UAE (Fujairah) and six IRGC boats sunk; Brent +5.5% to ~$114 (2022 high), TTF ~EUR49; UST10 4.456% (high since July 2025); 80% for a June ECB hike, ~50% for a June BoE hike, ~15% for a December Fed hike.
- 5 May: Hegseth: the ceasefire holds, ships crossed with a destroyer; Brent ~$111; gilt 10Y 5.10% (2008 high) and 30Y 5.76% (1998 high) with nearly three BoE hikes and >3 ECB hikes priced; RBA hiked to 4.35%; Romania's government fell (leu record low ~5.2/EUR).
- 6-7 May: Rubio declared "Epic Fury" concluded, Trump paused the escorts, and a one-page US memorandum to end the war went to Iran via Pakistan; Brent -7.6% to ~$100.5 (6 May) and ~$97-100 (7 May), TTF EUR42.9, DXY back to pre-war ~97.7; UST10 4.32-4.35%, Bund below 3%, BTP-Bund 75bp; ECB and BoE pricing cut to ~two hikes (~50bp); CNB held 7-0 at 3.50%; Norges Bank hiked to 4.25%; Trump gave the EU until 4 July to cut tariffs to zero.
- 8-10 May: fresh US-Iran clashes in Hormuz, Trump says the truce holds; payrolls +115k (62k expected), UMich 48.2 (record low), Brent ~$101 (-7% on the week); a US trade court struck down the 10% global tariffs (7 May); Labour routed in UK local elections; Hungary's new PM Magyar elected, HU10Y 5.75% (27-month low); Iran replied on Sunday and Trump called it "totally unacceptable".
- 11-13 May: Brent back to ~$104-108 (Iran demands Hormuz sovereignty; Trump: truce "on life support"; EIA: Hormuz shut to end-May; IEA: record stock draws); US April CPI 3.8% (core 2.8%) and PPI +1.4% m/m pushed UST10 to 4.49% (July-2025 high) and December Fed-hike odds to 30-35%; Starmer's leadership crisis lifted the gilt 10Y to 5.11% (18-year high); Bund 10Y 3.10% with three ECB hikes priced (June 80-86%); Warsh confirmed as governor (12 May) and chair (13 May, 54-45); Trump arrived in Beijing on 13 May; Romanian April CPI 10.7% and Q1 GDP -1.7% y/y.
